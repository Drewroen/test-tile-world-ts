      How to play

      • Collect the chips shown in the HUD, then reach the exit — doors and hazards need the right keys and boots.
      • Arrow keys or WASD move Chip; on a touch screen, tap near the edge of the board in the direction you want.
      • The clock only starts on your first move, so take your time at the start of a level.
      • R restarts the level. Best times are saved in your browser, per level and ruleset.
